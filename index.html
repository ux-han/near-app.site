What it isn't

Not another social network. Not another dating app. Not another location app.

It sits where the three overlap, and the promise is the thing none of them offer: there are interesting people around you, and now there's a way to meet them. It isn't trying to replace real-world interaction. It's trying to make it easier to start.

The whole product is organised by three distances. Scroll, and the ring grows — the nearer something is, the darker it's drawn.

The first move

A wave says I'd be up for meeting — and costs almost nothing.

OpenYou're open unless you turn it off: people at the places near you can see you're at the café, and can wave. You wave back and a chat opens. If you don't, nothing happens — and nobody is told.

Drawn, not trackedPeople sit on venues, not points. near knows which café you're in and nothing finer than that — no precise positions, no paths, no "last seen". Off is one tap: you vanish from every map, nothing is kept.
